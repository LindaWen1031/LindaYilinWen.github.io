## Context

Research teams and product teams fail in different directions. Describe the
specific friction: researchers optimising for a novel result, engineers for a
system that stays up, designers for an experience that makes sense, and a
roadmap that quietly assumed all three would converge on their own.

## My role

I was the connective tissue. Not the person with the best idea in the room — the
person responsible for making sure the best idea in the room actually became the
thing we built. Be concrete about the mechanisms you introduced.

## What we did

- Replaced status meetings with a shared decision log, so disagreements got
  written down and resolved rather than relitigated.
- Introduced a single quality bar that researchers, engineers, and designers all
  had a hand in defining — which is why it held.
- Ran a monthly session where each discipline explained a recent decision to the
  others. Slow at first, then the fastest thing we did.
- Made the roadmap legible: what we are certain about, what we are betting on,
  and what we have explicitly parked.
