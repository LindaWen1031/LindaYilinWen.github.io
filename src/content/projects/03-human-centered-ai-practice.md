rarely about discovering something nobody could have known; it is about checking

## My role

I designed and ran the research, and then — crucially — I was accountable for
what happened to the findings. Name that second half explicitly. Plenty of
research is well-run and ignored; the interesting part is the translation.

## What we did

- Ran contextual interviews in the settings where the tool would actually be
  used, not in a lab.
- Built a deliberately rough prototype so participants would tell us it was
  wrong, rather than politely agreeing it was right.
- Synthesised with the engineers in the room, so the findings landed as shared
  understanding rather than a report nobody read.
- Turned the top findings into explicit product principles that we could hold
  later decisions against.
